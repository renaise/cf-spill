The rule

Every record pairs a public claim with a document anyone can open. No source, no record. Rumor does not enter the archive.

Records are about statements, never about people. Names are optional for the person filing; the source carries the weight.

Status

Status describes the claim against the documents. It never describes the person.

SUPPORTEDA document corroborates the claim.
UNSUPPORTEDNo document found either way. The honest default, and not an accusation.
CONTRADICTEDA named document states something incompatible.
UNCHECKABLENo public document could settle it.

Why there are no counts

No likes, no scores, no ranking, no trending. A record either has a source or it does not, and a crowd cannot vote a document into existence. Ranking claims by popularity would also make this a publisher rather than a record.

What is not here

No claims about private individuals. No words about intent. You cannot source what someone meant, so it never gets published.

Reporting a record

Every record carries a report control under the ••• menu. A report names what is wrong with the record, and it is read by a person. Records found to be unsourced or misattributed are removed.
