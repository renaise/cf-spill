Spill Mobile prototype

The claim is public. The document should be too.

Founders state revenue, headcount and funding in public, at volume, with nothing attached. When someone does check, the work lands in a group chat and is gone by morning.

Spill keeps it. One record per claim, the source attached, open to anyone.

Running live

This is the real app in a 393 × 852 frame, not a picture of it. Every record in it is fictional, which is why the app says so in a band it will not let you close.

  • Tap a claimOpens the record: the full document, and what would change its status.
  • ChipsFilter the list by status. There are no votes and no ranking.
  • +File a record. It refuses to submit without a source.
  • ⋯Copy, share, or report a record.

You are on a phone already, so there is no frame to put it in.
