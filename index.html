Cracked

The claim is public. The receipt should be too.

Founders state revenue, headcount, and funding in public, at volume, with nothing attached. Most of it is never checked by anyone.

When someone does check, they spend twenty minutes in the Delaware registry, on Wayback, in an archived job post. Then they paste the result into a group chat of nine people and it is gone by morning. The next person starts from zero.

Cracked keeps that work. One page per claim, the source attached, open to anyone.

How it works
A claim and a source Every post pairs the statement with a document anyone can open. A filing, a cache, an archive link.
No source, no post Rumor does not enter the record. The rule is the moderation policy and it is not negotiable.
Names are optional Post without one. The source carries the weight, so the author does not have to.
